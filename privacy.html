Privacy Policy

QuietLoom Worship · Last updated 1 September 2026

1. Scope

QuietLoom Worship is a private publishing tool used by one person — the channel owner — to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own social media accounts. It has no public users and no sign-up.

2. What we collect

We do not collect personal information from any third party. The application never requests, receives or stores data belonging to other people's accounts.

When the owner authorises the application against their own TikTok account, TikTok returns:

  • an access token and refresh token;
  • an account identifier (open_id);
  • basic profile information for the owner's own account, such as display name.

3. How it is stored

These credentials are written to a single file on the owner's own computer and are used only to upload the owner's videos. They are not transmitted to any third party, not sold, not shared, and not used for advertising, profiling or analytics. They exist on that one machine and nowhere else.

4. How it is used

Solely to authenticate uploads of the owner's own content to the owner's own account. The application performs no other action against the TikTok API.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the application is in use. They are deleted by removing the local credentials file, and access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok account's connected-apps settings, which immediately invalidates them.

6. Cookies and tracking

These pages set no cookies and run no analytics or tracking of any kind.

7. Children

The application is not directed at children and has no users other than the owner.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date above reflects the current version.
